Built for NIT Srinagar Students

One Platform. All Campus Services.

Campus Nexus is a decentralized, privacy-preserving digital ecosystem connecting students across hostels, departments, and transit routes.

Graduated Student Verification

Every student account starts with campus access, while critical services like the student directory and ride coordination require institutional verification to protect student privacy and safety.

Zero Commercial Middlemen

Commercial platforms exploit students with surging commissions and high fares. Campus Nexus enables direct peer-to-peer taxi and flat sharing with transparent splits.

Modular Infrastructure

Built as an umbrella platform. Active with COORDINATE and ACCOMMODATE today, designed so any student builder or research group can create and plug in new services.

Open Invitation to Campus Builders

Platform Architecture

The Nexus Architecture

How independent campus microservices unite under a unified identity, security boundary, and real-time event pipeline.

Centralized Single-Sign-On

Passkeys, biometric hardware security, and enrollment-based authentication work seamlessly across every present and future module.

Privacy & Safety Gates

Strict role-based access control, cryptographic verification tokens, and session revocations prevent unauthorized access.

Unified Messaging & Events

A persistent WebSocket backbone delivers real-time notifications, peer chat, and ride departure updates with zero polling overhead.
