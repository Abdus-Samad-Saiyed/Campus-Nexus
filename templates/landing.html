Campus Nexus Early-Launch & Verification

The platform was originally supposed to release with all accounts requiring physical verification before activation to prevent fake accounts and enforce accountability.

However, due to the approaching end of semester, student need was prioritized and the platform has been released in early-launch, allowing for self-declared Quick-Bypass Accounts that do not require verification.

These accounts will get a chance for physical verification at the earliest in the following semester (Spring 2027). Until then, services can be availed using these temporary Quick-Bypass Accounts.
