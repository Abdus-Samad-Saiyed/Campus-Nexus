Install Campus Nexus on iOS

Install Campus Nexus on your iPhone or iPad for fullscreen access, faster navigation, and native app experience:

  1. Tap the Share icon in Safari's bottom toolbar.
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add in the top-right corner.

Campus Nexus will appear on your Home Screen as a standalone app with its own icon.

Install Campus Nexus

Install Campus Nexus for lightning-fast fullscreen coordination, 2-week persistent passkey sign-in, and offline safety features:

On Desktop (Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera)
  1. Look at the right end of your browser's address bar (URL bar).
  2. Click the Install Campus Nexus icon.
  3. Click Install when prompted.
On Android (Chrome, Samsung Internet, Edge)
  1. Tap the three dots Menu in the top-right corner.
  2. Select Install app or Add to Home screen.
  3. Tap Install to add Campus Nexus to your device.
On Mac (Safari & Chrome)
  1. Safari: In the top menu bar, click File → Add to Dock... and click Add.
  2. Chrome / Edge / Brave: Click the Install icon at the right of your address bar.
